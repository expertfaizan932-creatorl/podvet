Help Center

Frequently asked questions

Everything about starting a free trial, the referral card, pricing and your data.

How does the free trial work?

Every new clinic gets one full month free — no card required. You unlock everything PodVet does: medical records, vaccinations, appointments, billing, inventory and reports.

When the month ends, your clinic is paused until you choose the Standard plan at Rs 3,000 per month.

What is a referral card and how do I use the code?

Inside PodVet you can generate a referral card carrying a unique code. Give the card to another clinic — they scan the QR code with their phone, or type the code manually on the signup page, and it is applied automatically.

Using a valid code at signup gives the new clinic 50% off (Rs 1,500) its first invoice. Each card is valid for one month from the day it is printed.

What does the Standard plan cost?

The Standard plan is Rs 3,000 per month after your free trial, billed in Pakistani Rupees (PKR). There are no hidden setup fees, and you can stop at any time.

If you signed up with a referral code, the 50% discount is applied to your first invoice automatically.

Can I move my data from another system?

Yes. Our team helps migrate patients, medical history, inventory and client records from spreadsheets or another practice-management system. Contact us and we'll plan the move with you.

Who can use PodVet?

Solo veterinarians, multi-doctor clinics, mobile vets and animal hospitals. Each team gets one clinic workspace with unlimited clients and pets, plus separate logins for owners and staff.

Is my data private and safe?

Yes. Medical records are encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up continuously, and never sold or shared. You can export your data any time. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

How does the AI help in day-to-day work?

PodVet drafts SOAP notes from your exam findings, suggests reminders for vaccinations and follow-ups, and surfaces client balances. The AI assists with the paperwork — the clinical decisions stay with you.

What if I need help or the trial expires and I can't log in?

Write to support@podvet.com or use the Contact page. If your free month has ended your clinic is paused automatically — once you renew, everything is restored exactly as you left it.

We'll be happy to help you personally.
