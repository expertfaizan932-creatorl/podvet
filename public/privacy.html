Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who we are

PodVet ("we", "us") provides veterinary clinic management software. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using PodVet you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details — clinic name, your name, email, username and password (stored as a one-way hash).
  • Clinic data — patients, pets, medical records, vaccinations, appointments, invoices and inventory that you enter into PodVet. This data belongs to your clinic.
  • Usage data — technical logs such as IP address, browser and device type, used to keep the service reliable and secure.
  • Referral data — referral codes you generate and redeem so the 50% referral discount can be applied.

3. How we use information

  • To provide and maintain the service you signed up for.
  • To apply the free trial, subscription pricing and referral discounts correctly.
  • To send service messages (billing notices, trial-expiry reminders, product updates).
  • To improve PodVet and keep it secure, and to support you when you contact us.

4. What we never do

  • We never sell your data or your clinic's patient records.
  • We never use patient records to train advertising systems.
  • We never share your clinic data with other clinics or partners.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in controlled, secured databases with continuous backups. Access is restricted to authorised staff on a need-to-know basis. You can export or delete your data at any time.

6. Cookies and local storage

PodVet uses cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences and measure basic usage. You can clear these from your browser at any time; signing out may remove some stored data.

7. Data retention

We keep your account and clinic data for as long as your clinic is active. If you close your account, we delete your data within 30 days unless we are legally required to keep it (for example, for invoicing records).

8. Your rights

  • Access and correct the personal information we hold about you.
  • Export your clinic data in a common format.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where lawfully possible.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced inside the app or by email at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued use of PodVet after the changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to support@podvet.com or use our Contact page.
