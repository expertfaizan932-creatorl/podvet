Getting Started

1. Sign up free

Create your account at /signup. You get a 1-month free trial of everything in Standard - no card required.

2. Set up your clinic

Add your clinic profile, staff and permissions in Settings. Our team helps with onboarding and offers a setup call before your trial ends.

3. Import or add data

Bring in clients, pets, services and products with Excel/JSON import, or enter them directly. Ask us for free migration help from your old software.

Appointments & Daily Flowboard

The schedule is a per-vet daily flowboard. Open Appointments and press "new appointment": find an existing patient or add a new client + pet, then pick the pet, doctor, date and time. Add services and/or products and any notes - the status defaults to Confirmed.

  • Categories. Consultation & General Care, Preventive Care, Diagnostics, Specialized Care, Surgery, Grooming, Boarding and Other.
  • Row actions. Edit, cancel, open the Medical Record, preview/reprint the invoice, Complete Payment, print the POS slip, Deduct from stock, add Custom Reminders or open WhatsApp.
  • Filters & status. Payment filters cover Paid / Partially Paid / Unpaid / Billed. Discounts can be percent or fixed plus a coupon.
  • Auto-scheduler. The scheduler can fill empty slots so no consultation time goes to waste.

Clients & Patient Records

One profile per client holding every pet, invoice balance, visit history and preferences. Add a client and attach each of their pets (species, breed, birth date, sex, weight and medical notes).

Patient profile tabs

Age, weight history, appointments, medical records, vaccinations, allergies, medications, documents and more - everything about the pet in one screen.

Stay connected

Spot clients who haven't visited in months and re-engage them in one click, or send SMS/WhatsApp messages straight from the profile.

Medical Records & SOAP Notes

Medical records are SOAP-based: Subjective, Objective, Assessment and Plan - with AI-assisted drafting to save typing. Each record includes an examination checklist, exam systems, weight, temperature, a Body Condition Score (BCS), diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions and lab tests.

Record types. Consultations, follow-ups, vaccines, surgeries, boarding, grooming and other service types - each with its own template.
Prescriptions. Draft and export prescriptions as PDF with your clinic branding.
Boarding. Consent forms, daily/hospitalization summaries, feeding and medication notes, plus a checkout invoice.

Vaccination Tracker

Track every vaccine across the whole patient list. PodVet computes when each booster is due, highlights overdue pets and sends automatic SMS & WhatsApp reminders on schedule - so patients come back exactly when they should.

Inventory & Vendors

Manage products, stock levels and purchase orders. When you sell or use a product on an appointment you can Deduct from stock (with quantity) right from the appointment row - keeping inventory always in sync.

Vendors & consignment. Record vendor details, track consignment stock that isn't owned yet, and know what to reorder before you run out.
Stock views. Filter products, see low-stock items and expiry-sensitive products, and spot slow movers.

Billing, Invoices & Payments

Invoice directly from appointments or the quick bill screen. PodVet tracks Paid, Partially Paid, Unpaid and Billed states, and you can print the invoice, the POS slip or reprint any document.

Customisable. Percent or fixed discounts, coupons, a loyalty card, and an optional first-time customer fee (default Rs 1,050) that you can change in Settings.
Payment proofs. Clients can submit payment proofs; you review and reconcile them from the payment history.

SMS & WhatsApp Messaging

Send appointment reminders, vaccination boosters and follow-ups over SMS or WhatsApp using ready-made templates. Delivery receipts and opt-out handling are automatic, and when a client replies the thread stays attached to their file.

Reports & Analytics

Understand your clinic's performance: revenue, appointment loads, payment statuses and patient activity. Every report can be exported (including monthly expense lists as PDF) and statements follow your own receipt branding.

Settings & Data

Users & roles. OWNER, ADMIN and USER permissions control who sees and does what in the clinic.
Clinic branding. Add your logo, accent colour and bank details to invoices and documents.
Data management. Export all your data as Excel, or import JSON/XLSX/XLS. Your plan and payment history live under Current Plan.
