First 30 days with a puppy: a vet's guide

PodVet Editorial · September 2026

SM

Dr. Sana Malik, DVM

Lead Veterinarian & PodVet Medical Editor · Sep 10, 2026

Bringing home a puppy is joyful and a little chaotic. The first month sets the pattern for years of behaviour and health, so getting the basics right early matters more than almost anything else you'll do together.

Week 1: settle in

Give the puppy a quiet corner, a consistent feeding schedule and plenty of sleep — puppies need up to 18–20 hours a day. Book a first vet visit within the first week for a health check, deworming and the first vaccination. Keep introductions to other pets and new people gradual and supervised.

Weeks 2–4: socialisation window

The critical socialisation window runs roughly from 3 to 16 weeks. Expose your puppy to different surfaces, sounds, people and friendly, fully vaccinated dogs. Puppy classes that follow safe protocols are ideal. Under-socialised puppies grow into anxious or reactive adults — this window is your best chance to build a confident dog.

House training that sticks

Take the puppy out after every nap, meal and play session. Reward the behaviour, not the puppy. Never punish accidents — just clean and refocus. With a predictable schedule, most puppies are reliably housetrained within a few weeks.

Vaccination timeline

Your vet will build a schedule from about 6–8 weeks, with boosters a few weeks apart. Until the full course is finished, avoid parks and unvaccinated dogs. Write down every dose (and keep a photo of the vaccine card in PodVet's patient file) so nothing is missed.

Clinic tip

Vaccination reminders in PodVet automatically notify owners a few days before a booster is due. Clinics using reminders see fewer missed vaccines and more loyal clients — nobody has to remember "which week it was".

The takeaway

The first month is about routine, safety and positive experiences. Keep vet visits on schedule, socialise early and often, and be patient — you're building habits that will last your dog's whole life.
