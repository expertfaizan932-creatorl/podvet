About PodVet

Built by vets, for the team behind the counter

PodVet is veterinary clinic management software that lets you focus on your patients while we run the clinic — appointments, records, billing, inventory and reports in one place.

Our story

PodVet started with a simple frustration: veterinary teams were spending more time on paper files, booking books and spreadsheets than with their patients. Every visit meant rewriting the same notes into a dozen different places — and getting paid used to take days.

So we built one platform that holds the whole clinic together. Medical records, vaccinations, appointments, online invoicing, inventory and reports live in a single place, with an AI assistant that drafts your SOAP notes and reminders so nothing falls through the cracks.

Today PodVet powers small solo practices, busy multi-doctor clinics and animal hospitals — from the first free month to hundreds of thousands of patients on file.

Healthier, calmer clinics — that's the whole point.
